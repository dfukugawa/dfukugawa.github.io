Breakout

    Open the game on its own page

    Bounce the ball off your paddle and break all 50 blocks. Each block you break before the ball returns to the paddle earns more points, starting at 100, then 150, then 200. On clearing the board, you’ll see your score, completion time, and remaining lives.

    • Move with your mouse or trackpad.
    • Launch or resume with a left click, Space, or Enter.
    • Pause with a right click or Esc.
